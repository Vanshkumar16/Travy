Company: Microsoft Corporation
Role: Data Analyst
Location: Hyderabad, India
Work Mode: Hybrid

About the role
The Customer Success Analytics team turns business data into insight for Microsoft's cloud customer operations. As a Data Analyst you will help leaders understand what is happening in the business and why.

What you'll do
• Write advanced SQL queries, stored procedures and views to transform raw business data into reporting tables.
• Build and maintain Power BI dashboards and reports used by senior leadership for weekly business reviews.
• Investigate data discrepancies and work with engineering teams to protect data integrity across pipelines.
• Track key performance metrics and explain the drivers behind changes to business stakeholders.
• Automate recurring data cleaning tasks using Python.

What we're looking for
• Bachelor's degree in Analytics, Computer Science, Economics, Statistics or a related field.
• Strong proficiency in SQL and data modeling.
• Hands-on experience with Power BI or Tableau and Microsoft Excel.
• Introductory Python or R for data analysis.
• Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.
• Excellent written and verbal communication and presentation skills.

Nice to have
• Familiarity with Azure data services and data warehousing.
• Basic understanding of statistics and A/B testing.
