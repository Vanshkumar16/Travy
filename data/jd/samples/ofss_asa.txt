Company: Oracle Financial Services Software
Role: Application Support Analyst
Location: Bengaluru / Chennai, India
Work Mode: Onsite
Experience: 0-2 years

Overview
Our Application Support team keeps core banking applications running for global banking customers. You will be the first line of technical response when something goes wrong in production.

Responsibilities
• Monitor banking batch jobs and troubleshoot production exceptions within strict SLAs.
• Analyze application logs on Linux servers and execute diagnostic SQL queries to find the root cause of issues.
• Communicate clearly with banking clients during incident triage and provide written root cause analysis reports.
• Follow ITIL incident and change management processes and maintain runbooks.
• Coordinate with development teams to escalate defects and validate fixes.

Requirements
• Degree in Computer Science, IT or a related technical field.
• Working knowledge of SQL queries and relational databases (Oracle preferred).
• Familiarity with Linux shell commands and basic shell scripting.
• Basic Java knowledge to read stack traces and understand application errors.
• Strong analytical and logical reasoning skills with a structured troubleshooting approach.
• Excellent client communication skills and willingness to work in shifts.

Preferred
• ITIL Foundation certification.
• Exposure to banking or financial services applications.

About Oracle
Oracle FSS serves banks in more than 140 countries. Benefits include health cover and shift allowances.
